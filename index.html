Hands

The main camera is shown on screen and sets where the shape is. More cameras see your hands from other sides and make distances much more accurate.

    Add a phone as a camera

    Open the link or scan the code on the phone. Phones need this page over HTTPS (npm run dev:lan), and both devices need internet: the public PeerJS server introduces them, then hand data goes directly between them.

    Calibrate the cameras and hands

    Calibration makes distances right. It is saved for these cameras in this browser. With several cameras, calibrate their positions: hold up one hand and move it through the space they all see.

    Hold the left button to put your right hand in (Shift: left hand). The wheel moves the hand nearer or farther. Right-drag turns the orbit view.

    Placement

    Keys: M menu, S show shape, P place at hands, Esc close. Use headphones: your left hand plays in your left ear.

    ShapeSonic 3D

    An invisible 3D shape floats in front of you. Explore it with your hands and listen: a soft sound guides you toward it, each fingertip plays its own note inside it, and edges and corners pluck and ring.

    Use headphones. Your left hand plays in your left ear.
